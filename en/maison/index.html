About us

We are flavourists.We create emotions.

Founded in 2013, our Marseille laboratory brings together qualified chemists and flavourists. From formulation to analysis, from flavour matching to made-to-order production, we support every client at every stage.

Expansion

Four sites, soon just one

Spread across four sites today, the laboratory will bring all its activities together in 2027 in a single 3,000 m² hub.

A purpose-built site: formulation laboratory, analysis room, production and packaging lines, regulation-compliant storage. Designed from the floor plan up around the real workflows of the trade, and for the neighbourhood and cost constraints that come with them.

Production

Everything is made here

Our products are made entirely in our own laboratory, in the south of France. Full traceability, from raw material to delivered bottle.
