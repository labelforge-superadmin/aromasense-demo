Method

Molecular formulation

Building a flavour note by note, from its elementary constituents.

Just as a painter selects colours, a flavourist chooses raw materials. A flavour combines different olfactory notes: a heart that carries the main note, a bouquet that brings roundness and richness. Thousands of formulas developed since 2013 make up our working library.

Method

Mixology

Blending, dosing, balancing: the flavourist’s craft.

Mixology starts from existing flavours, which it combines and adjusts. It is the method built for responsiveness: it lets us put forward a credible direction within days, then refine it through iterations with the client.

Method

GC-MS reproduction

Analysing a profile down to its molecules, then rebuilding it.

Extraction, concentration, separation and identification of the odour-active compounds behind a flavour. It is flavour matching at the molecular scale, and the only method that leaves measurable proof of what it claims.

From A to Z

Eight stages, one point of contact

Many flavourists practise all three creation methods. What is rare is to carry a project from end to end: from the first idea to a product that is labelled, declared and ready to sell, whether it is a syrup, a spirit or an e-liquid.

  1. 01

    Formulation

    Molecular formulation, mixology, GC-MS reproduction, flavour matching, range extensions.

  2. 02

    Analysis

    Extraction, concentration, separation and identification of odour-active compounds. Study of complex raw materials.

  3. 03

    Production

    Flavour concentrates and ready-to-use liquids, from prototype to large-scale production, to your specifications.

  4. 04

    Quality control

    A protocol defined project by project, from physico-chemical testing to sensory evaluation.

  5. 05

    Regulatory

    TPD notifications, CLP classification, safety data sheets, INRS and poison centre notifications, regulatory monitoring.

  6. 06

    Packaging

    Plastic or glass bottles, outer packaging, bulk. Any run, any format, from the bottle to the ten-litre drum.

  7. 07

    Labels & cartons

    Design and printing of multi-SKU self-adhesive labels, cartoning, complete packaging.

  8. 08

    Client equipment

    Producing in-house? We supply the equipment, automate dosing and support you through commissioning.

Flavour matching is part of the craft: faithfully reproducing an existing profile, with our own raw materials and under our regulatory responsibility. We talk about it openly.
